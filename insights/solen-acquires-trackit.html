Trackit joins Solen Software Group

10 March 2026 · 1 min read

Solen Software Group has acquired Trackit, LLC, a provider of transit-specific software for safety plan compliance, recordkeeping and cross-department collaboration. The transaction closed in February 2026.

Trackit is used at over 100 transit locations. Its platform addresses the administrative requirements of the Public Transportation Agency Safety Plan rule, work that most agencies otherwise run on spreadsheets and shared drives.

Trackit will continue operating with autonomy, with Solen supporting the business through long-term ownership and optional shared services, allowing the team to remain focused on product development and customer support.

Mark Anderson continues as President.
